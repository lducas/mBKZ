The content of this folder accompanies Footnote 4 in our paper, supporting our choice to view the case K=Q in Fig. 1 as a baseline for comparing module-BKZ with unstructured BKZ. 

The results of running `allexp_slope.sh` (in the main folder) are illustrated in `unstruct_slopes.pdf`, showing that the slopes of progressive BKZ over various number fields are indistinguishable up to statistical noise. 
This confirms that (unstructured) BKZ does not see the module structure, as usually assumed in the literature.
